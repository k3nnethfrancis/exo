# Code of Conduct

We want Stem to be a constructive place to build local-first tools together.

Be respectful, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Do not
harass, discriminate, threaten, publish private information, or disrupt the
community. Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate
these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. We will
handle reports with discretion and make a good-faith effort to protect people
who raise concerns.
